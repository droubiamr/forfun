# Homs Walk: prompt

Copy everything below into Claude. To make your own city, change the city, landmarks, character and coordinates.

---

Build a self-playing pixel-art street walk of **Homs, Syria** for the web. Use only `index.html` + `main.js`: no libraries, no build step, and draw every image in code on a canvas.

- **The walk:** a little pixel man (slicked-back dark hair, full dark beard, black suit, white shirt, green tie) starts walking on page load and never stops. The street loops seamlessly back to the start.
- **The street, in order:** the old cast-iron clock, Al-Dablan Street shops (manakish oven, a hummus shop with blue barrels of chickpeas out front, sweets, coffee), the New Clock tower, the covered souq (turquoise lattice roof, bunting, lanterns), Old Homs (black basalt walls, cream arches, striped windows), and the Khalid ibn al-Walid Mosque (silver domes, two minarets). Add the citadel hill with its transmission towers in the skyline.
- **Two stops:** at the hummus shop he says "صحن حمص… بحمص!" (a plate of hummus… in Homs). At the New Clock he takes a selfie: only his face lights up from the flash. A polaroid then slides in, drawn front-on: him smiling with the clock behind him.
- **Live Homs:** use the real Homs time (Asia/Damascus) and real sun and moon positions for the sky, stars and lit windows. Take the weather from Open-Meteo (no key needed). The clocks in the scene show the real time.
- **Music:** a soft, original lo-fi loop in maqam Bayati (oud, ney, darbuka). It's on by default and starts on the first tap.
- **Screen:** full screen with no edges. The place name in Arabic and English sits top-left. Top-right has small buttons: pause, sound, and "Homs now" (time, weather, try another time). On phones the buttons stack.
- **Tone:** warm and hopeful.
